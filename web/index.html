Demo script

  1. Honest shopper: in the phone app pick Aisle 7, take a razor on the shelf simulator, scan it, go to exit, tap at gate: green.
  2. Forgot to scan: take a chocolate and don't scan it. After the match window the phone gets a nudge.
  3. Barcode swap: take blades, then scan the gum barcode. The engine flags the swap.
  4. Pocketed item: take a razor, ignore the nudge, go to exit: red, staff check appears on the ops console.
  5. Network down: on the ops console switch the cloud offline, then exit a clean trip: the gate still verifies the signed token.

Tip: start the server with TRUSTLANE_MATCH_WINDOW=20 so nudges arrive faster during a demo.
